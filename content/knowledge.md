## FAQ

Q: Is Aruba safe?
A: Aruba is one of the safest destinations in the Caribbean. Average crime rates are low
compared to other islands, and the people are very friendly. Aruba also sits just outside
the hurricane belt, so it's rarely in the direct path of a storm.

Q: What is the weather like in Aruba?
A: Aruba has the least rainfall of any island in the Caribbean. The temperature stays
around 28°C (82°F) all year, and the sun is almost always shining aside from the
occasional shower — one reason the island has such a high percentage of repeat visitors.

Q: What can you do in Aruba?
A: Plenty beyond the beach. Visit historical Oranjestad and its museums, or head to San
Nicolas ("Sunrise City") for its colorful murals. Explore Arikok National Park and the Ayo
and Casibari rock formations, take a catamaran out to a shipwreck along the coast, or go
parasailing for a different view of the coastline.

Q: What is the best time to visit Aruba?
A: Aruba is warm year-round, so it's a good trip any month. High season runs
mid-December through mid-April, with the most visitors and the highest prices — for a
quieter, more affordable trip, aim for the summer months instead.

Q: What is the best place to stay in Aruba?
A: Palm Beach is the most popular area, home to most of the island's high-rise hotels,
casinos, restaurants and watersports operators. Eagle Beach's low-rise hotels suit a
quieter stay, and Oranjestad, a bit further from the beach, makes up for it with color
and character.
